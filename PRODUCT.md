## Users

People who decide about sales performance in German-speaking B2B companies: managing directors, sales leads, and the salespeople and self-employed sellers themselves. Second audience: leaders in financial and wealth-advisory sales who build teams but have no time to train new advisers on the phone. They arrive from LinkedIn, Instagram, a phone call with Fares, or the Coach im Ohr site, mostly on a phone, and decide within one screen whether this man knows more about selling than they do.

## Product Purpose

The personal site of Fares Chahrour, sales trainer and mentor, under his brand Vertriebszirkel. It shows who he is and what he does, and it gets the right visitor to book a free 30-minute first call (Calendly). Fares wants the site to be about his own story and sales coaching, nothing else (Feedback meeting, 20.11.2026).

Success: a sales lead books the first call already believing that Fares has done the job longer and better than anyone they would otherwise hire.

## Positioning

- **Practice, not theory.** In sales since 1999 (27 years in 2026) and still doing B2B cold acquisition himself every day. He trains what he does that morning.
- **The phone is his instrument.** Opening, needs analysis, the next step. Fares' 5 V: Vorbereiten, Verstehen, Verbinden, Vereinbaren, Verbessern. ABC: Ausgangslage, Zielbild, Hindernisse.
- **Financial and wealth-advisory sales from the inside.** 2000 to 2019 Geschäftsstellenleiter at the Deutsche Vermögensberatung, built and trained his own sales structure. This is exactly where Coach im Ohr runs its first pilot.
- **Asks instead of pushes.** "Klarheit verkauft stärker als Druck." He does not talk a customer into a problem; he finds out together whether there is a relevant one.

## Operating Context

- One action everywhere: the free 30-minute first call, booked through Calendly (`calendly.com/fareschahrour/vertriebs-klarheitsgespraech`), with a privacy notice next to it.
- Three ways in (from the current site): VertriebsPerformance Check (management and sales leads), Vertriebsrad (salespeople and self-employed, eight personal areas, interactive self-assessment), PraxisPerformance 90 (90 days for salespeople and teams).

- Ezencia (Felix Rohde, felixrohde.com) built the site and is credited in the footer; Ezencia lists the site in its works.

## Product Principles

1. Show the practitioner, not the seminar: every claim traces back to something Fares does or did.
2. One action, one door: the first call. Everything else serves it.
3. Ask before telling: the site asks the visitor questions the way Fares does on the phone.
4. Fares is the person; Coach im Ohr is the tool; Ezencia is the builder. Each links the other, none swallows Fares.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Many visitors are over 45 and on a phone: body text at least 17px, high contrast, tap targets at least 44px.
